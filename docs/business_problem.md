# E-Commerce Sales and Customer Analytics

## Background
This project is about an online store that sells furniture, office supplies and technology products. The store gets a lot of orders, but the owner doesn't know which products and customers actually make profit. They also give a lot of discounts and don't know if the discounts help or not. I'm using the order data to find answers to these things.

## Goal
To find out which products, categories and customers bring the most revenue and profit, where the store is losing money, and what the business can do to grow.

## Who will use this
- Owner: wants to know if the business is growing and making profit
- Marketing team: wants to know which customers are valuable and which ones stopped buying
- Category managers: wants to know which products make profit and which make loss
- Finance team: wants to know how discounts affect profit

## KPIs I will use
| KPI | Formula | Why it matters |

* Revenue ----> Sum of Sales  
(shows how big the business is.)

* Profit----> Sum of Profit 
( money the business actually keeps.)


* Profit Margin----> Profit / Revenue x 100 
(shows how much profit we get from every 100 rupees of sales, so we can compare big and small categories fairly.)


* Total Orders -----> Unique Order ID count 
(number of orders placed.)


* Total Customers ----> Unique Customer ID count 
(size of customer base.)

* AOV ----> Revenue / Total Orders 
(how much a customer spends in one order.)


* Repeat Customer Rate ----> Customers with 2+ orders / Total Customers x 100 
(shows how many customers come back.)

## Questions I want to answer

**Sales**
- What is the total revenue, profit and profit margin?
- How do sales and profit change month by month? Is there a season where sales go up?
- Which 3 months have the best sales and which 3 have the worst?

**Products**
- Which 10 products give the most profit?
- Which categories have high sales but low profit margin?
- Which products or sub-categories are making a loss?
- How does discount affect profit?

**Customers**
- Who are the top 10 customers by revenue and how many orders did they place?
- What percentage of customers ordered more than once?
- How many customers have not ordered in the last 90 days of the data?
- Which customer segment gives the most revenue?

**Customer behavior**
- How many customers are new and how many are returning?
- Can I group customers using RFM (recency, frequency, monetary)?

## Decisions this can help with
- If a category has high sales but low profit because of high discount, the business can put a limit on the discount.
- If most customers ordered only once, the business can send them offers so they come back.
- If a region has high sales but still loses money, the business should first check the reason (discount, shipping cost or product type) and then fix it.
- If a few customers give most of the revenue, the business should take care of them with special rewards, because losing them would hurt a lot.
